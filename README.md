# 🗝️ Das Elsewhere — A Little Universe on the Other Side of Ordinary

*A strange little journey through the planets, where every mystery hides a discovery.*

What if exploring the universe felt like stepping through a tiny door into another world?

**Das Elsewhere** is a Coraline-inspired, interactive space-exploration experience that blends astronomy, mystery, and a whimsical, slightly eerie storybook atmosphere. It invites visitors to travel between planets, uncover clues, solve scientific challenges, and discover what lies beyond the familiar.

With deep indigo skies, dusty-pink stars, peculiar floating objects, and secrets waiting to be uncovered, Das Elsewhere turns learning about space into something a little more magical — and a little more mysterious.

Because sometimes, the most interesting discoveries begin when you dare to look beyond the ordinary. 🌙

## 🗝️ The Inspiration

Since its the spooky season, i got inspired by the uncanny charm and handmade storybook atmosphere of *Coraline*, I wanted to create a world that feels familiar at first glance, but just a little strange when you look closer.

I was drawn to the idea of hidden doors, curious objects, secret pathways, and worlds that invite you to explore further. I wanted to bring that feeling into a space-themed experience, combining an eerie, whimsical aesthetic with real scientific discoveries.

Rather than making another conventional educational website, I wanted to create an experience that feels like a story unfolding. Every planet becomes a new destination, every clue brings you closer to an answer, and every scientific challenge gives you another reason to keep exploring.

**Das Elsewhere is where my love for science meets storytelling, imagination, and the delight of discovering something unexpected.**

## 🌌 What Can You Discover?

- 🪐 **An interactive solar system** — explore eight planetary destinations, beginning with Mercury.
- 🔐 **Progressive planet unlocking** — complete missions to unlock the next planet and continue your expedition.
- 📖 **Mystery-driven missions** — uncover short stories and follow clues that guide you through your journey.
- 🔭 **Scientific challenges** — answer multiple-choice questions inspired by real astronomy and planetary science.
- 🧩 **Clues and discoveries** — connect the pieces of each mission to understand the world you're exploring.
- 💡 **Learning through explanations** — discover the science behind each challenge, including why an answer is correct.
- 💾 **Saved progress** — completed missions are saved in your browser, allowing you to return to your expedition.
- 🌠 **A peculiar storybook atmosphere** — floating decorations, stars, mysterious details, and a dreamy, slightly unsettling visual style.
- 🌙 **A secret ending** — finish the final planetary mission to uncover the conclusion of your journey.

## 🚀 Your Expedition Begins Here

Your journey takes you through the eight planets of our solar system, from Mercury to Neptune.

Each destination has its own scientific theme and mission, inviting you to discover something new about the universe.

| Planet | A glimpse of the mystery |
|---|---|
| ☀️ Mercury | The strange extremes of planetary heat |
| 🌫️ Venus | The greenhouse effect and a world of intense heat |
| 🌍 Earth | The conditions that make our planet habitable |
| 🔴 Mars | The search for water and clues to the planet's past |
| 🟠 Jupiter | The composition of the solar system's largest planet |
| 🪐 Saturn | The science behind its magnificent rings |
| 🩵 Uranus | The unusual tilt of a distant ice giant |
| 🔵 Neptune | The powerful winds of the outermost planet |

Your expedition unfolds one mission at a time. Read the story, investigate the clues, and answer the scientific challenge to progress to the next destination.

Complete the final mission on Neptune to reveal the secret ending.

## 🎮 How to Play

### 1. Step into Elsewhere

Open the experience and select **Play Now** to enter the mysterious universe.

### 2. Begin on Earth

Earth is your starting destination. Explore the available planet information and discover your first mission.

### 3. Follow the clues

Each mission introduces a small mystery. Read the story carefully and look for clues that can help you understand what's happening.

### 4. Solve the scientific challenge

Answer the multiple-choice question using the clues and your understanding of the science involved. Each challenge is an opportunity to learn something about our solar system.

### 5. Unlock the next planet

Complete a mission to progress through the expedition and unlock the next destination.

### 6. Reach the end of the journey

Continue through all eight planetary missions, from Mercury to Neptune, to discover the final secret waiting at the end.

**A little tip:** Don't rush through the stories. The clues are part of the experience, and the science is part of the mystery. 🗝️

## 💻 How to Run Locally

Want to explore the project on your own computer?

### What you'll need

- A modern web browser
- Git, or the option to download the project as a ZIP file
- Visual Studio Code (recommended, but optional)

### Option 1 — Clone the repository

Open a terminal and run the following commands, replacing the repository URL with the current URL from your GitHub page if it has changed:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/niamaty22-ship-it/Das-Elsewhere.git
cd Das-Elsewhere
```

Open the downloaded folder in Visual Studio Code.

### Option 2 — Download the project

1. Open the project's GitHub repository.
2. Select **Code → Download ZIP**.
3. Extract the downloaded archive.
4. Open the extracted project folder.

### Launch the experience

The project is built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

You can open `index.html` in your browser, or use the **Live Server** extension in Visual Studio Code to run it through a local development server.

Keep the `assets` folder and the project's HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files together so the images, decorations, and interactive features can load correctly.

**Note:** Mission progress is saved in your browser's local storage. Progress saved in one browser or on one device won't automatically transfer to another.
